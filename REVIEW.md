# Review of the weekly summary pull request

List the problems you found, most serious first. Copy the block below for each one.

### 1. One-line summary of the problem

- **Where:** file and line
- **What goes wrong:** a concrete example: what someone does, and the wrong result they get
- **Severity:** critical, high, medium or low
- **Fixed:** yes (name the commit) or no

## Anything else

Questions you'd ask the author, or anything you'd want checked before this is merged.

## How you worked

- **Time spent:** roughly how long it took, including setup.
- **AI tools:** "None", or which tools you used, what you used them for, and what you checked or changed in what they gave you. This doesn't affect your score.
